Meme Coin Tycoon FAQ

Meme Coin Tycoon is a free Telegram game where you trade memecoins and real cryptos with virtual money. Launch your own coin, create your own blockchain, mine, stake and climb the leaderboard. Nothing in the game has real money value.

🎮 Play on Telegram
💡 Everything in the game is virtual money: it has no real value and cannot be withdrawn.

💵 How do I start?

  • You start with $1,000 of virtual cash.
  • Claim 🎁 $200 every day (top of the screen). Day 7 of your streak gives $1,000.
  • Join @memecointycoon: +$500 once, then a secret code worth +$150 every day.
  • Finish the 8 "Getting started" steps and claim a $3,000 reward.

📈 How do prices work?

  • Real cryptos (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Toncoin) follow their real price.
  • Memecoins follow a curve: every buy pushes the price up, every sell pushes it down.
  • Recent coins are the cheapest: early buyers win the most.
  • News (celebrity tweets, whale dumps, hacks) moves the prices.

🚀 How do I launch my own memecoin?

  • Pick a name, a ticker, a logo and a color. It costs $500, up to 3 coins a day.
  • Choose the blockchain: compare its fees and its launch grant.
  • Buy your own coin first to get the lowest price, then share it.
  • You earn a creator fee (0% to 2%) on every trade other players make on your coin.

🎓 What is graduation?

  • A memecoin that passes a $10,000 market cap graduates to the DEX and is announced on the channel.
  • Its creator wins $2,000 once 10 other players hold it, if he still holds some himself.

🚨 What is a rug pull?

When a creator dumps at least half of his own coins, the coin is marked 🚨 Rug for 24 hours and loses its graduation reward. Be careful with coins whose creator sells a lot.

💀 What are dead coins?

Coins with no trade for 7 days, or almost worthless, move to the 💀 Dead tab. You can still buy and sell them, and they come back to the market if they come back to life.

⛓️ What is a blockchain in the game?

  • Every coin lives on a blockchain that takes a fee on each of its trades.
  • Create your own blockchain for $3,000 and set its fee (0.1% to 10%).
  • You earn that fee on every trade of every coin launched on your chain.
  • Optional launch grant: you pay it to each player who launches a coin on your chain, to attract creators.

⛏️ How does mining work?

Buy a rig and pick a coin: it mines while you are away. Upgrade the CPU, GPU and cooling. Like in real life you pay electricity, it gets harder when many players mine the same coin, and rewards halve over time. Collect before your rig overheats.

🔒 What is staking?

Lock coins you hold to get more back: +1% in 1 day, +10% in 7 days, +50% in 30 days. You can unstake early, but then you get no interest.

🎯 Orders and ⚡ futures

  • Limit buy, take-profit, stop-loss and price alerts work even when you are away.
  • Futures: bet on the rise (Long) or the fall (Short) of real cryptos with x2, x5 or x10 leverage. If the loss reaches 90% of your margin, you are liquidated.

🏆 How does the leaderboard work?

Your net worth = your cash + everything you own (coins, staking, open positions). Cash bought with Stars does not count: only what you earn with it counts.

👥 Invite friends

  • You and each friend get $500.
  • Referral contest: $1,000 for the best referrer of the day; $5,000 / $3,000 / $1,000 every week; $15,000 + 👑 crown for 30 days / $7,000 / $3,000 every month.
  • Only friends who make at least one trade count. Prizes are paid automatically.

⭐ What can I buy with Telegram Stars?

  • ⭐ Recharge: +$500 of virtual cash for 10 ⭐, once a day (does not count in the ranking).
  • 📣 Promote a coin: top of the market for 3 hours + a post on the channel, 25 ⭐ (3 coins at most at the same time).
  • 📣 Promote your blockchain: first in the list for every coin creator for 24 hours + a post, 25 ⭐ (2 at most at the same time).
  • 💎 Supporter pass, mining boosts and streak saves.

Everything is optional: the whole game is free to play.

❓ Is it real money?

No. All dollars, coins and blockchains in the game are virtual. They have no real money value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money. Stars only buy in-game extras.
