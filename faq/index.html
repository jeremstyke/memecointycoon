FAQ

Meme Coin Tycoon is a free Telegram game where you trade memecoins and real cryptos with virtual money. Launch your own coin, create your own blockchain, mine, stake and climb the leaderboard. The game cash and coins have no real money value.

💡 The cash, coins and blockchains of the game are virtual money: they have no real value and cannot be withdrawn. Only the small 💎 TON rewards are real.

💵 How do I start?

  • You start with $1,000 of virtual cash.
  • Claim your daily bonus from the bonus card at the top of the Market screen. It grows with your streak: $200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 500, then $1,000 on day 7. Missing a day resets the streak.
  • Join @memecointycoon: +$500 once, then +$100 more on your daily bonus every day while you stay subscribed, and a secret code worth $150 to $500 every day.
  • Finish the 8 "Getting started" steps and claim a $1,500 reward.

📈 How do prices work?

  • Real cryptos (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Toncoin) follow their real price.
  • Memecoins follow a curve: every buy pushes the price up, every sell pushes it down.
  • Recent coins are the cheapest: early buyers win the most.
  • News (celebrity tweets, whale dumps, hacks) moves the prices.

🚀 How do I launch my own memecoin?

  • Pick a name, a ticker, a logo and a color. It costs $500, up to 3 coins a day.
  • Choose the blockchain: compare its fees and its launch grant.
  • Buy your own coin first to get the lowest price, then share it.
  • You earn a creator fee (0% to 2%) on every trade other players make on your coin.

🎓 What is graduation?

  • A memecoin graduates to the DEX when it passes a $10,000 market cap and at least 10 players other than its creator hold it. It is then announced on the channel.
  • Its creator wins $2,000 if he still holds some and the coin was not rugged.

🚨 What is a rug pull?

When a creator sells at least half of his own coins in one sale, the coin is marked 🚨 Rug for 24 hours and loses its graduation reward, and the creator cannot launch a new coin for 24 hours. Be careful with coins whose creator sells a lot.

💀 What are dead coins?

Coins with no trade for 7 days, or almost worthless, move to the 💀 Dead tab. You can still buy and sell them, and they come back to the market if they come back to life.

⛓️ What is a blockchain in the game?

  • Every coin lives on a blockchain that takes a fee on each of its trades.
  • Create your own blockchain for $3,000 and set its fee (0.1% to 10%).
  • You earn that fee on every trade of every coin launched on your chain.
  • Optional launch grant: you pay it to each player who launches a coin on your chain, to attract creators.

⛏️ How does mining work?

Buy a rig and pick a coin: it mines while you are away. Tap "Upgrade your rig": each tap improves its cheapest part. Like in real life you pay electricity, it gets harder when many players mine the same coin, and rewards halve over time. Collect before your rig overheats. Mining boost: double your mining for 1 hour for 5 ⭐, as often as you want (one at a time), plus 1 free boost a day by watching a video.

🔒 What is the Vault (staking)?

Lock coins you hold in the 🔒 Vault to get more back: +1% in 1 day, +10% in 7 days, +50% in 30 days. You can unstake early, but then you get no interest.

🎯 What are daily missions?

Every day, 3 missions wait for you at the top of the Market screen (make trades, stake, collect your mine, launch a coin...). Each one pays $50 to $100, and finishing all 3 opens a bonus chest worth $200. New missions every night at midnight (Paris time).

🏆 How does the leaderboard work?

Your net worth = your cash + the coins you hold + the coins in your 🔒 Vault. Cash bought with Stars does not count: only what you earn with it counts.
