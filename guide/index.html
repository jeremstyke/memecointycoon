Tips from the pros

📈 Trading

Buy early

The price of a new coin goes up with every buy. The first buyers get the lowest price, so check the 🆕 Recent tab every day.

Never go all-in

Split your cash over 3 or 4 coins. One bad coin should never wipe out your whole wallet.

Take your profits

A coin that doubled can halve just as fast. Selling part of your bag on the way up locks in real gains.

Do not chase pumps

When a coin already went up +200% today, late buyers often become the exit for early ones. Look for the next one instead.

Holders matter

A coin held by many players is harder to crash than a coin held by 2 people. Check the holders count before you buy big.

Watch the news

A celebrity tweet can pump a coin, a market fear can drop everything. Read the news on the Market screen before you trade.

Use limit orders

Set a price and let the game buy or sell for you when it is reached. No need to watch the chart all day.

Patience pays

Most big wins come from coins bought early and held a few days, not from trading every minute.

Real crypto tab

The 🌍 Crypto tab follows the real prices of Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Toncoin. Practice your trading with zero risk.

🚀 Your own coin

Launch your own coin

Create your coin, buy it first at the lowest price, then share it. Every buyer after you pushes your bag up.

Pick the right blockchain

Before launching a coin, compare the blockchains: lower fees attract more traders, and some owners pay you a launch grant.

Creator fees: keep them low

As a coin creator you can take 0 to 2% on every trade. AI buyers prefer coins with low creator fees, so greed can kill your volume.

Migration

You can move your coin to another blockchain once every 7 days for $500. Useful if a cheaper chain appears.

Spot a rug pull

If the creator dumps half of his coins at once, the price crashes and a 🚨 Rug alert appears. Coins with many holders are usually safer.

Graduated coins

A coin with the 🎓 DEX badge has reached a big market cap. It is usually less volatile than a brand new coin.

Dead coins can come back

Coins with no trades for 7 days end up in the 💀 Dead tab. Some players buy them for almost nothing and wait for a revival.

⛓️ Blockchains

Own a blockchain

Every coin launched on your blockchain pays you a fee on each buy and sell, even the coins launched by AIs.

Low fees attract more coins

Blockchains with low fees get picked more often by players and AIs. A small fee on many trades beats a big fee on none.

Launch grants work

Offering a launch grant on your blockchain convinces creators to launch there. Keep enough cash so the grant stays visible.

⛏️ Mining and staking

Your mine works while you sleep

Claim your mine before it fills up: once it is full, it stops earning. A bigger storage lets you sleep longer.

Upgrade your mine early

Each mining upgrade raises what you earn every hour, for good. The earlier you upgrade, the more it pays back.

Mining boost

Once a day you can double your mining for 1 hour (5 ⭐). Use it right after you claim, when your mine is empty, to get the full hour.

Staking = more coins

Lock coins you believe in for a few days and get more of them back at the end. Perfect for coins you would hold anyway.

⚡ Futures

Futures are risky

Leverage multiplies your gains and your losses. If the price goes the wrong way too far, your position is liquidated and the margin is lost.

Start small with futures

Try futures with a small margin and low leverage first. Learn how fast prices move before you bet big.
