## What is it?

Meme Coin Tycoon is a free trading game that runs inside Telegram as a mini app. Every player can create a memecoin and trade the coins of other players. Prices move with real demand from the players: every buy pushes the price up, every sell pushes it down.

> Virtual coins only. There is no real money in the game: you cannot deposit, withdraw or cash out anything.

## How to play

1. **Create your coin**: pick a ticker, an emoji logo and a color.
2. **Buy early**: the first buyers of a coin win the most when others buy after them.
3. **Share and climb**: invite friends to buy your coin and climb the leaderboard.

## Features

- 💵 $1,000 of virtual cash to start
- 🎁 A free $200 bonus every day
- 📈 A live chart for every coin
- 🏆 Leaderboards: top players, top coins, biggest gainers
- 🤖 AI traders (clearly marked) that keep the market alive
- 📣 Share links that open a coin directly in the game
